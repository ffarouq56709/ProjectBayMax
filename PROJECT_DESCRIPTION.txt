ProjectBayMax is Faisal Farouq's solo, one-year build of a friendly caregiver-support research robot: 1.829 m tall, rounded white panels, a connected-eye face and a warm voice. Year one aims for a body of 55 kg or less with ten powered leg joints, two supervised three-step tethered trials at 0.10-0.15 m/s, and a separate stationary rig that moves a 2 kg inert object ten times. Software covers companionship, fictional care records, medication logging and approved mock messages. People stay in charge. Nothing is built or tested yet. Qualified help joins for specific reviews.
