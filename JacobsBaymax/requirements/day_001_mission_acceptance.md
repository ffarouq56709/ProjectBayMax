## 2. Proposed mission - read and confirm this wording

**M01.** Jacobs Baymax will be developed as a caregiver-support research prototype for companionship, engagement, fictional information organization and communication; the human caregiver retains responsibility, authority and clinical judgment.

**M02.** The intended year-one result is a controlled research demonstration of the six outcomes below, with separately reviewed evidence and explicit limitations; completing all six within a year remains an unproven goal.

**M03.** The physical design targets a height of 1.829 m, an operating robot mass of no more than 55 kg including the battery, panels and attached modules, and exactly 10 active lower-body joints; the joint arrangement still requires engineering review and design freeze.

**M04.** A projected mass above 55 kg requires rework; a projection above 65 kg requires a major design review and suspension of affected purchases and motion releases.

**M05.** The total year-one cash ceiling is USD 10,000, with USD 5,000 as the lower funding case; funding availability, prior spending and a technically feasible full-system budget have not been confirmed.

**M06.** The intended appearance uses rigid removable white plastic panels, visible narrow seams, protected charcoal joint gaps, subtle teal accents, a simple connected-eye face and broad flat rounded-rectangle feet, subject to motion, cooling, service and safety access needs.

## 3. Exclusions and operating boundaries

**X01.** Use fictional profiles and simulated health information only; do not store or transmit real patient information or connect to a live electronic health record in this initial prototype.

**X02.** Do not diagnose, recommend treatment, interpret clinical scans or claim disease detection.

**X03.** Do not prescribe, select, dispense or administer medication, calculate doses, change medication instructions autonomously, or suggest double doses or schedule changes.

**X04.** Never lift, catch, transfer, restrain or physically support a person.

**X05.** Do not walk with the handling test object or other cargo; the 2 kg handling demonstration is stationary and mechanically secured.

**X06.** Do not permit unsupervised high-energy motion or unapproved public operation; physical work requires the applicable reviewed configuration, permits, qualified people and prior releases.

**X07.** Do not claim professional-care replacement, emergency dispatch, successful human review or an emergency-response guarantee.

**X08.** Do not use an inflatable exterior or combat, superhero or military styling.

**X09.** Do not report a robot function as passed without identifiable executed test evidence and reviewer disposition; planned targets and document checks are not performance results.

**X10.** Conversational AI must not bypass the independent safety controller or directly authorize hazardous motion, safety-limit changes or recipient changes.
