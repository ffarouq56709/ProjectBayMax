# Document presentation preference

Effective 2026-10-27. Accountable owner: Faisal Farouq.

For future readable project documents and PDFs:

- Omit tool/model authorship credits and drafting-process footers, including credits that name an assistant or describe assistance.
- Omit time-entry wording and personal reporting qualifiers from footers, badges and document bodies.
- Keep operational role/time entries in structured evidence records when required by the daily tracker.
- Retain useful project content, accountable owner, date, revision, source references and actual result labels.
- Continue the approved cute Baymax notebook style.

This preference supersedes earlier presentation conventions. Earlier archived evidence is preserved.
