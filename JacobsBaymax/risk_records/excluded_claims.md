# Excluded claims

- The robot shall not diagnose, recommend treatment, interpret clinical scans or claim disease detection.
- The robot shall not prescribe, select, dispense, administer, calculate doses or alter medication instructions autonomously.
- The robot shall never lift, catch, transfer, restrain or physically support a person.
- The robot shall not promise emergency dispatch, successful human review or professional-care replacement.
- Early development shall not store or transmit real patient information or connect to live EHRs.
- The project shall not use inflatable exterior, combat, superhero or military styling.
- No unsupervised high-energy motion or unapproved public operation shall be permitted.
- No function shall be reported as passed without identifiable executed test evidence and reviewer disposition.
